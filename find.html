How to read this. Each cell is roughly 1.4 miles across and shows the median depth of wells filed inside it. Cells holding fewer than three wells are left out entirely rather than smoothed over — a median drawn from one or two wells is an anecdote, and interpolating across empty desert would invent confidence that the records don't support. Blank areas on this map mean no data, not shallow water.

A neighbouring well is not a promise about your parcel. Depth can change by hundreds of feet inside a single section, and drilling outcomes depend on local geology and luck. Use this to narrow where to look, then get a quote from a licensed driller before you buy.

Source: Arizona Department of Water Resources, Wells55 registry (2024 full export). Depths filtered to 20–5,000 ft to exclude data-entry errors and boreholes.
