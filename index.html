Honest about what this is: only the water estimate is currently built from your location. Septic, power and access use published statewide ranges and don't yet respond to your parcel. Every line is tagged so you can see which is which.

Well depths, static water levels and record counts come from the Arizona Department of Water Resources — the Wells55 registry for county summaries, and the Groundwater Site Inventory for the live radius lookup. Cost ranges reflect published 2026 Arizona figures: drilling $25–75/ft depending on formation, complete well systems $20,000–50,000 installed, conventional septic $7,500–15,000, alternative/ATU systems $20,000–40,000, cisterns $8,000–20,000 installed, and hauled water $200–350 per 1,000 gallons.

This is an estimate, not a bid. Drilling outcomes vary with local geology and luck — neighbouring wells can differ by hundreds of feet, and a nearby record is not a promise about your parcel. Get a quote from a licensed driller and confirm septic requirements with the county before you buy.
